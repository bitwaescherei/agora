# Welcome to the [[Bitwäscherei Agora]]!

**This [[Agora]] is a [[Knowledge Commons]] maintained by the [[Bitwäscherei]] community and friends.**

Running internally at [agora.init5.ch](https://agora.init5.ch) (and eventually accessible at [bit.agor.ai](https://bit.agor.ai)).

This Agora integrates notes, wikis, and knowledge bases from multiple community repositories:

- [[Bitwäscherei]]: Community notes and hackerspace documentation.
- [[SGMK]]: Schweizerische Gesellschaft für Mechatronische Kunst.
- [[Hackteria]]: Open Source Biological Art and DIY science.
- [[Idiot.io]]: Open hardware and IoT wiki archives.
- [[TAMI]]: Tel Aviv Makers wiki archives.
- [[Flancian]]: Digital garden of Flancian.
- [[Flancia]]: Essays and resources from flancia.org.
- [[Agora Doc]]: Agora documentation and collaborative stoas.

**If you would like to join or contribute to this Agora, please refer to [[Agora signup]] or submit a pull request adding your repository to `sources.yaml`.**

Thank you for visiting this Agora. May you be happy!
